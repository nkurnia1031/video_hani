On-screen text
04D (akhir): Psychology is more than viral terms. It needs context, evidence, and careful understanding.
Motion / camera
04A: emosi berubah menjadi self-label. 04B: label berpindah ke teman dan meretakkan hubungan. 04C: satu label membesar menjadi stigma; kartu konteks mengitari orangnya. 04D: label terurai menjadi konteks, bukti, dan pemahaman cermat; teks layar tampil di akhir.
Script / voice-over
Now, why should we care? First, misusing psychological terms can make us misunderstand our own emotions. Normal feelings like sadness, fear, or disappointment can feel like something is seriously wrong with us. Second, it can create unnecessary conflict. Imagine labeling someone as narcissistic just because they disagree with you. And third, it can reinforce stigma. Mental health conditions are complex experiences, not simply labels we can attach to someone based on one behavior. Remember, psychology is more than just a collection of viral terms. It is a scientific discipline that requires context, evidence, and careful understanding.

How to Use Therapy Speak Wisely

A checklist illustration sits beside three practical steps and short Indonesian explanations.

Download editable SVGDownload PNG preview
On-screen text
Understand before you label · Describe, don’t diagnose · Learn, don’t self-diagnose
Motion / camera
Tiga baris checklist dan ikon sederhana muncul satu per satu.
Script / voice-over
So, how can we use psychological terms more wisely? Here's your three-step reminder. Number one, understand before you label. Cari tahu makna suatu istilah sebelum menggunakannya. Number two, describe behavior, not diagnose people. Daripada langsung menyebut seseorang narcissist, lebih baik jelaskan perilaku spesifik yang membuat kita merasa tidak nyaman. Number three, know your limits. Konten media sosial bisa menjadi sumber edukasi awal, but it cannot replace professional assessment. And most importantly, allow yourself and others to experience normal human emotions without immediately turning them into disorders.
