Visual audit

Storyset illustrations share the same flat Rafiki vector language, blue #407BFF, dark ink #263238, warm neutrals, and soft shadows. Custom backgrounds and labels use that palette; each scene changes its composition to match its action.

Editing & attribution

Open or download each SVG to edit its vector layers and text. The source bundle contains the HTML board and all scene SVGs. Storyset art is free with attribution; use the per-scene links and review Storyset terms for final use.

The presenter frame is a footage slot. Custom scene backgrounds, chips, labels, article cards, faces, and icons are hand-built SVG shapes; no images were generated. Storyset illustrations are embedded as SVG groups and remain vector artwork.
