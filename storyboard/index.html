Script / voice-over
That's also why ethical responsibility matters when we talk about psychology. Dalam Kode Etik Psikologi Indonesia, ada beberapa prinsip yang relevan dengan fenomena ini. Pasal 9 menekankan bahwa keputusan psikolog dan ilmuwan psikologi harus didasarkan pada pengetahuan ilmiah dan sikap profesional yang telah teruji. Artinya, psychological claims should not be based on assumptions or viral trends alone. Pasal 15 membahas penghindaran dampak buruk. Sementara itu, Pasal 31 menekankan bahwa pernyataan melalui media harus konsisten dengan kode etik dan didasarkan pada pengetahuan serta praktik psikologi yang tepat. So, sharing psychology is not just about being relatable. It's also about being responsible.

How to Use Therapy Speak Wisely

A checklist illustration sits beside three practical steps and short Indonesian explanations.

Download editable SVGDownload PNG preview
On-screen text
Understand before you label · Describe, don’t diagnose · Learn, don’t self-diagnose
Motion / camera
Tiga baris checklist dan ikon sederhana muncul satu per satu.
Script / voice-over
So, how can we use psychological terms more wisely? Here's your three-step reminder. Number one, understand before you label. Cari tahu makna suatu istilah sebelum menggunakannya. Number two, describe behavior, not diagnose people. Daripada langsung menyebut seseorang narcissist, lebih baik jelaskan perilaku spesifik yang membuat kita merasa tidak nyaman. Number three, know your limits. Konten media sosial bisa menjadi sumber edukasi awal, but it cannot replace professional assessment. And most importantly, allow yourself and others to experience normal human emotions without immediately turning them into disorders.
